# Aparajit

Aparajit is the system-software home for the AJIT and APARAJIT SPARCv8 processors. The purpose is a toolchain, NuttX and CoRTOS ports, a PX4 path, and QEMU, plus the documents needed to plan a sovereign Pixhawk-compatible autopilot.

The porting team builds the toolchain, the RTOS ports, and the PX4 path. Drone specialists judge flight-control and airframe fit. Chip specialists design the AJIT electronics and need the autopilot requirements. Investors decide whether to fund a Pixhawk-compatible autopilot.

Chip RTL, fabrication, and proof of third-party silicon datasheets are out of scope.
