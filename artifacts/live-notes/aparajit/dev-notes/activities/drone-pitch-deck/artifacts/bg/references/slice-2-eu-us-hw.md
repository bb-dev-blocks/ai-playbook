## Draft citation verdicts

- **"AirBrain H743 Datasheet v2.5 (Gear Up / EUnify BV, Issued June 22, 2026)"**: **exists** (R30). Title, publisher and date are correct.
  - Supported: "MCU STM32H7, 480 MHz, 2 MB flash, 1 MB RAM" (p.7); "Battery input 3-10S LiPo, 10-42 V" (p.8); "Operating temp 0 - 60 °C" (p.8, p.21); "Storage temp -20 to 70 °C" (p.21); "Onboard storage Winbond W25N01, 128 MB NAND" (p.7); "NDAA compliant" (p.1); and CE: "EUnify BV declares conformity with EMC Directive 2014/30/EU and RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU (as amended by 2015/863)" (p.25).
  - The datasheet itself says only "STM32H7". The exact part (STM32H743VIH6) comes from the GitHub README.
  - Unsupported: "~0.2 mW/MHz / ~96 mW at 480 MHz / 2.0 mW/MIPS / 0.83 mW/MFLOPS". The datasheet has no CPU power figures, and ST DS12110 contradicts them (see next item).
  - Misleading framing: 0–60 °C is the **board** rating. The STM32H743 silicon itself is rated −40 to +85 °C ambient (R31).
- **ST STM32H743 datasheet (DS12110)**: **exists**; Rev 11, January 2026 (R31).
  - "frequency up to 480 MHz … 1027 DMIPS/2.14 DMIPS/MHz" (p.1). "The maximum CPU frequency of 480 MHz can be obtained on devices revision V" (Table 1, note 3).
  - 480 MHz requires VOS0, which has max TJ = 105 °C (Table 113, p.210). Above that, VOS1 is limited to 400 MHz.
  - Run-mode current figures are in Table 119 (p.215) and Tables 120–121 (p.216); see Verified metrics.
- **"EU Defence Industry & Space C(2025) 8719 final (Annex 3) (European Commission, Dec 17, 2025)"**: **exists**. Number and date are correct. It was amended by C(2026) 690 final (4.2.2026) and by "amendment #1" (landing page dated 30 Apr 2026).
  - Annex 3 is the **call topic descriptions** (R43); the work programme proper is Annex 1 (R32). Citing "Annex 3" for call list or budgets is imprecise; cite Annex 1.
  - Total: "maximum Union contribution … EUR 1 005 978 500" (Art. 2): 13.0201 capability development EUR 676 099 033, and 13.0301 defence research EUR 329 879 467.
- **Q&A claim "€110M research budget"**: **claims differ**. EUR 110 000 000 is the budget of the **EDF-2026-RA call** only ("EUR 110 000 000 for seven call topics", Annex 1 p.6). Total defence-research appropriation is EUR 329 879 467.
- **EDF-2026-LS-DIS-RA-SMERO-NT**: exists. "Non-thematic research actions by SMEs and research organisations".
  - Budget EUR 35 000 000; max EUR 5 000 000 per proposal; duration 12–36 months; lump sum; indirect management by EDA (Annex 1 §3.3.3, p.26).
  - Consortium must be SMEs or research organisations, with an SME coordinator; research organisations ≤40% of the requested grant (Annex 1 p.7).
  - Portal: opened 2026-04-15; **deadline 2026-09-29; status Closed** (as of 2026-10-05).
- **EDF-2026-LS-DA-SME-NT**: exists. "Non-thematic development actions by SMEs".
  - Budget EUR 30 000 000; max EUR 6 000 000 per proposal; lump sum; EDA (§3.3.4, p.26–27).
  - Must cover at least one of design, prototyping, testing, qualification, certification or increasing efficiency.
  - Portal: opened 2026-02-11; deadline 2026-09-29; Closed.
- **EDF-2026-LS-DIS-NT**: exists. "Non-thematic actions targeting disruptive technologies for defence".
  - Budget EUR 27 000 000; max EUR 3 000 000 per proposal; 12–24 months (§3.3.5, p.27–28).
  - Portal: opened 2026-04-15; deadline 2026-09-29; Closed.
  - Some activities are funded at 100%: "activities … under the EDF-2026-LS-DIS call and aim to … increase efficiency … (Article 10.3(i)) will be funded at 100%" (p.34).
- **EDF-2026-RA-MCBRN-DST**: exists. "CBRN decontamination systems and technologies". Budget EUR 15 000 000, under the EDF-2026-RA call (actual-cost grants) (§3.1.1.3, p.10). Portal: opened 2026-02-11; deadline 2026-09-29; Closed.
  - Annex 3 (R43 p.4–6) supports: RAS ("An appropriate level of robotic and autonomous systems (RAS) is required in MDPs"), a "decision support tool", "self-decontaminating materials", and UxV as an optional item ("Proposals should also address: … analysing, developing and testing unmanned vehicles (UxV) payload capabilities for MDPs").
  - Draft "TRL3–TRL6" is **unsupported**. The text says "proof-of-concept (TRL3)" and "validating … (TRL5)".
  - Fit with a flight-controller chip is weak: the topic's core is decontamination (nanosorbents and decontamination procedures).
- **EDF-2026-LS-RA-CHALLENGE-DIGIT-AISAP**: exists in Annex 1 under this ID. **The portal lists the topic as "EDF-2026-LS-RA-CHALLENGE-DIGIT-AISAP-STEP".**
  - "AI-based tactical situational awareness using swarms of small robots and drones – participation in a technological challenge".
  - Budget EUR 23 000 000; max EUR 4 600 000 per proposal; target "up to … TRL 6" (§3.1.6.1, p.14).
  - Portal: opened 2026-02-11; deadline 2026-09-29; Closed.
- **All 5 targeted 2026 topics closed on 29 Sep 2026.** Any pitch claim must point to the **2027** work programme. The landing page lists an "Indicative Multiannual Perspective 2026-2027"; it was not downloaded.
- **EDF eligibility for an Indian company**: supported by Reg 2021/697 Art. 9 (R34, OJ L 170 p.164–165).
  - Art. 9(1): "Recipients and subcontractors involved in an action shall be established in the Union or in an associated country."
  - Art. 9(3): "shall not be subject to control by a non-associated third country or by a non-associated third-country entity."
  - Art. 9(4): an EU-established entity controlled from a third country is eligible "only if guarantees approved by the Member State … in which it is established … are made available to the Commission". The guarantees must cover control, access to sensitive information, and IP/results staying within the recipient, not "exported outside the Union … without the approval of the Member State".
  - Art. 9(6): cooperation with non-EU entities is allowed, but "The costs related to those activities shall not be eligible for support from the Fund."
  - ⇒ An Indian parent cannot be a recipient. An EU subsidiary needs Member-State guarantees, and IP export to India is restricted.
- **"EASA C-class … (C1–C4 operational categories under EU 2019/947)"**: **claims differ**.
  - Class (C0–C6) product requirements are in **Delegated Reg 2019/945** (Annex Parts 1–6, 16, 17).
  - 2019/947 sets the operating rules (open/specific/certified categories), not the class definitions.
  - C5/C6 were added by 2020/1058 (R37).
- **CE / EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS 2011/65/EU (+2015/863)**: directives exist (R38, R39). R39 is the original 2011 text; the 2015/863 amendment was not downloaded.
- **"NDAA Section 848"**: **exists** (PL 116-92 §848, 133 Stat. 1508). Flight controllers have been named since the enacted 2019 text: "(B) uses flight controllers, radios, data transmission devices, cameras, or gimbals manufactured in a covered foreign country or by an entity domiciled in a covered foreign country".
  - The original "covered foreign country" was "the People's Republic of China" only.
  - As amended by PL 117-263 §817 (23 Dec 2022), it covers PRC, Russia, Iran and DPRK. It also adds (b): no DoD contract "on or after October 1, 2024, with an entity that operates … equipment from a covered unmanned aircraft system company".
  - Codified at 10 U.S.C. 4871 note (formerly 10 U.S.C. 2302 note).
  - India is not a covered country, so an Indian flight controller is not barred by §848.
- **"NDAA … 1260H"**: not verified in this slice (out of scope).
- **American Security Drone Act 2023** (PL 118-31 §1823): bars executive agencies from procuring a UAS "manufactured or assembled by a covered foreign entity, which includes associated elements … (consisting of communication links and the components that control the unmanned aircraft)".
  - Covered foreign entities are those on the FASC list, including "(D) Any entity domiciled in the People's Republic of China…".
  - Flight controllers are covered implicitly ("components that control the unmanned aircraft"), not by name.

## Gaps

- The deck's STM32 power numbers (0.2 mW/MHz, 96 mW) have no source and conflict with DS12110 (≈0.76–1.0 mW/MHz typical at 480 MHz, derived). Remove them or re-derive from R31. Any Ajit-vs-STM32 power comparison must state its conditions (VDD, code location, TJ).
- All 5 EDF-2026 topics closed on 29 Sep 2026. The 2027 work programme / Indicative Multiannual Perspective 2026-2027 was not downloaded or checked.
- Art. 9 of Reg 2021/697 is quoted from the 2021 OJ text. Changes by Reg (EU) 2025/2653 were not checked.
- 2019/945 and 2019/947: newer consolidations may exist and were not checked. The RoHS 2015/863 amending directive was not downloaded.
- §848 is a DoD-only restriction; ASDA covers federal agencies. Neither applies to EU procurement. "NDAA compliant" is a US marketing label in an EU pitch.
- NDAA §1260H: not verified.
- ARAI's original AIS-140 amendment PDF was not found; only a Kerala MVD compiled copy.
- The NavIC smartphone "mandate by 2025" was only a 2023 ministerial statement. PIB (Dec 2025) says it is not mandated. Do not claim a mandate.
- Only 3 NavIC satellites were broadcasting navigation signals as of Mar 2026. This weakens "dual-band NavIC moat" claims.
- MOSIS pricing and TSMC full-MPW prices: not found (TSMC prices are behind a request form).
- SCL MPW pricing: not public.
- PX4 has no SPARC target in its docs. NuttX does support SPARC SoCs (BM3803/BM3823/S698PM), which is a possible porting path.
